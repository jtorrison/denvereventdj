What I handle at corporate events

Every company event has a different job for the music. Here's what I'm usually doing.

Holiday parties

Welcome music, dinner, a few words from leadership, then a dance floor that gets the whole team involved, not just the usual five.

Company celebrations

Milestones, anniversaries and launches. I can cue walk-up music, run a reveal moment and keep the program on schedule.

Client and team events

Campus and community events

Big outdoor crowds, student events and public celebrations, with sound sized for the space and clean music throughout.

Corporate event questions

Can you provide mics for speeches, awards or a presentation?

Yes. I bring wireless mics and run the sound for speeches, toasts and awards. Send me the run of show and I'll cue walk-up music, keep the transitions tight and make sure the people in the back can hear.

Will the music stay work-appropriate?

Always. Corporate events get clean versions all night. You set the vibe, whether that's polished background music for networking or a full dance party after the awards, and I keep it right for the room.

Are you insured? Can you send our venue a certificate of insurance?

Yes. I carry liability insurance and my gear is insured. I can send a certificate of insurance (COI) to your venue ahead of the event.

Can you work with our venue's AV team or production company?

Yes. Outside the booth, I've spent years working with professional music and sound systems for businesses, so I'm comfortable plugging into a house system, coordinating load-in and power, or bringing a complete setup myself.

Can you do background music during networking and then a dance party?

That's the most common setup. Easy, conversational music while people mingle and eat, a clean handoff for speeches, then the energy goes up when it's time to celebrate.

Do you do outdoor company events and campus events?

Yes. I play campus events at CU Boulder, including crowds of about 3,000 students at Farrand Field, and I bring battery-powered sound for sites without power.

How do we book?

Send me your date, venue, headcount and what the event is for. I'll get back to you fast, usually with a call, and send a clear quote. A 50 percent deposit holds your date.
