How to use the planner

  1. Fill in what you know. Leave the rest blank or set it to "Let Josh pick." Most couples know their first dance and a few key songs, and that's plenty.
  2. Pick a direction for the dance floor. A few genres or eras tell your DJ more than a list of 50 songs.
  3. Keep must-plays short. Five to fifteen songs you'd be sad not to hear. Anything more turns the night into a playlist.
  4. Be clear on the do-not-play list. Songs, artists or whole genres. A good DJ treats it as a hard rule.
  5. Send it, print it or copy it. "Email my plan to Josh" opens your email app with everything filled in. You can also print it for your planner or paste it into a shared doc.

What to decide for each moment

  • Processional and entrance: timing matters more than the song. Check the length with the processional song calculator.
  • Recessional and grand entrance: big, joyful, starts strong.
  • Cocktail hour and dinner: a vibe, not a list. Conversation comes first.
  • First dance and parent dances: the songs that mean something. Too long? A custom edit fixes that.
  • Dance floor: a direction, a few must-plays and your do-not-play list.
  • Last dance and send-off: something everyone can sing.

Questions

Do I have to pick every song?

No. Pick the moments that matter to you and leave the rest to your DJ. Most couples choose their first dance, entrance songs and a handful of must-plays, and trust the DJ for everything else.

How many must-play songs should I list?

Five to fifteen is plenty. A short list guarantees your favorite moments and still leaves your DJ room to read the crowd.

What happens when I click Email my plan to Josh?

Your own email app opens with your plan in the message. Nothing is sent until you hit send, and nothing is stored on a server. Your answers are saved only in this browser so you can come back and finish later.

Can we change the plan later?

Of course. Plans change right up to the wedding. Couples I work with keep updating shared playlists and we check in before the day.

What if we're not working with you?

Use it anyway. Print it or copy it and send it to your DJ. It covers everything a good DJ will ask you.
