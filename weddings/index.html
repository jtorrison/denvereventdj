Wedding questions

Can you provide ceremony music and microphones?

Yes. I bring the speakers and wireless mics for your officiant and vows, and I set up wherever the ceremony is happening, with or without power nearby. I've run ceremony sound for more than 100 guests, and we'll size the setup to your crowd and your space. Ceremony coverage is part of the Full Experience and Extended Experience packages.

Can we send you playlists, requests and a do-not-play list?

Absolutely. Think of me as your music consultant for the whole night. We'll set up shared Spotify playlists. You can give me a direction and let me run with it, or hand me the exact songs you want and I'll mix them. Your do-not-play list is a hard rule. We'll also decide who gets to make requests: anyone, just the wedding party, or nobody.

What happens if our timeline or ceremony plans change?

I roll with it. You're hiring me to make the day work. At one wedding, everyone was already seated when a family emergency delayed the ceremony. I kept the music going and kept guests in the loop ("about five more minutes, folks") until everyone was ready. If plans shift while I'm on site, I adjust with them.

Do you coordinate with our venue, planner or band?

Yes, with everyone. Weddings have a lot of moving parts, and the music touches almost all of them. One wedding this summer had more than 25 vendors, and we all worked off the same plan. I'll connect with your venue, planner and band so every handoff is smooth.

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book about a year out. Right now, in October 2026, I'm booking weddings for next summer. Some plan even further ahead, and I already have a 2028 wedding in Las Vegas on the calendar. If your date is sooner, reach out anyway. If I'm open, I'm happy to make it work.

Do you MC? Who handles announcements, toasts, and the grand entrance?

I can handle as much or as little as you want. If a friend or your officiant wants to take the mic, great. If you want me to run it, I'll handle the grand entrance, call guests to dinner, release tables for food, gather everyone for the first dance, and keep toasts on track. Either way, the night flows and nobody is standing around wondering what's next.

How do you handle a mixed crowd, like grandparents and college friends?

This is where DJs really differ. Some play their own set no matter what's happening on the floor. I'm constantly reading the room and adjusting. If grandma gets up to dance, she's hearing a song she knows. Early in the night I start with upbeat, family-friendly music that gets everyone on the floor. As the crowd shifts later, so does the music. Slow songs go where they help the night, not where they kill it.

Can you do a custom edit for our first dance or entrance?

Yes. Send me the songs and ideas, and we'll hop on a call to plan it. I can blend multiple songs together, cut a song down to the right length, or remove a lyric you don't want playing in front of your family. Hear the custom first dance I made for the DelPiccolo wedding.

Can you play outdoors where there's no power?

Yes. I bring battery-powered speakers and wireless mics for ceremonies in places without an outlet, like mountain overlooks and backyards. We'll talk through your space during planning so nothing is left to chance.

What if we have a band?

I work with bands all the time. At a recent wedding at Boulder Flower Farm, I played the dinner set, Groove & Motion played their live set, and then I took over the dance floor through the end of the night, running through their system. I can cover cocktail hour, band breaks, the after-party, or anything in between, and I'll coordinate the setup directly with the band.

Do we meet before the wedding? How does planning work?

Once you book, I'll set up a call or Zoom to cover the basics: the vibe, your crowd, the venue, the timeline, and the music you love and hate. From there we'll have as many check-ins as you need. I'm happy to do a site visit, and I'll help with the details people forget, like whether your ceremony needs mics, speakers, or a power plan.

Do you bring lighting?

Yes. Standard lighting for the event is included. Beyond that it depends on the vibe you want. Some couples want clean white lighting only. Some parties want uplighting, lasers, and haze. We'll plan the right look together, and if you want something I don't own, I can rent it.
