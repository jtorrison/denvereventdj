Party questions

Do you bring lights?

Yes. Standard lighting is included. Parties usually want more, so I can add uplighting, lasers and haze to turn a room into a night out. We'll plan the look together, and if you want something I don't own, I can rent it.

Can you DJ a house party or backyard party?

Absolutely. I'll size the sound to your space so it feels like a party without rattling the whole block, and we'll talk through any neighborhood noise rules ahead of time.

Is the music clean for school dances?

Yes. School dances and family events stay clean all night. I've played homecoming, and I know how to keep a gym full of students dancing without a single surprise lyric.

Can you play a specific style, like house music all night?

Yes. Open format is my default, but house music is where I go when the night is mine. I perform club sets as NOSIRROT, so if you want a house or club night, that's my favorite kind of party.

Do you do Halloween and theme parties?

Every year. I can match the music to your theme, run a costume contest or announcements, and bring the lighting that makes it feel like an event.

Can you play outdoors?

Yes. I bring battery-powered speakers for spots without power, and I've played crowds of about 3,000 students outdoors at CU Boulder.
