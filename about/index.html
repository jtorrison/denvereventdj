Meet Your DJ

It started at an Apple Store

In middle school I discovered GarageBand on an iMac in an Apple Store. I didn't have a computer at home, so I'd make songs from loops in the store and come back whenever I could.

I saved money mowing lawns, shoveling driveways, cleaning offices and doing odd jobs until I could buy my own iMac. Then came Ableton Live, live loops and mixing for friends. By senior year, I had my first paid DJ gig at a Sweet 16.

Every crowd taught me something

The Hill in Boulder and college house parties taught me the power of familiar songs. Denver nightclubs taught me to build energy. School dances taught me to change direction fast when a track wasn't landing. Weddings taught me how to move between meaningful moments, dinner and a late-night party.

I still love the moment it clicks

I love DJing because every crowd is different. I pay attention to who's dancing and what the room needs. I like bringing generations together early and finding the right groove as the night changes. I mix live, adjust as I go and genuinely love the work.

When I'm not DJing weddings, I'm NOSIRROT

House music is where I go when the night is mine. I've played direct support for Cedric Gervais at Madame Ushi in Aspen, and I still play club and house sets whenever I can. It's the same skill as a wedding dance floor: read the room and keep it moving.

The technical side comes with me

Outside the booth, I've also spent years working with professionally curated music and sound systems for businesses. From a simple speaker connection to complicated multi-zone audio, I've helped people troubleshoot the technical details. That experience comes with me to every event.

Tell me what you're imagining. I'll help you figure out how to make it happen.

I can help with music for different moments, ceremony sound, microphones, vendor coordination and additional production when needed. You don't need to know exactly what equipment to ask for. Tell me the vision, and we'll work through a practical plan and quote together.
