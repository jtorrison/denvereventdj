Booking and pricing

How much does it cost to book you?

Packages start at $1,499 for up to four hours. Full Experience, which covers the ceremony through the reception for up to six hours, is $2,499, and Extended Experience, up to eight hours, is $2,999. Everything is laid out on the pricing page.

How do we hold our date?

Once we've confirmed the details and signed the contract, a 50 percent deposit holds your date. The deposit is non-refundable, and the rest is due the week of your event.

Can we keep the party going longer on the night?

Yes. Overtime is $250 per hour, it's written into the contract, and you can decide that night.

Do you price match?

Send me the other quote. I'll either beat it or walk you through exactly what's different, so you're comparing apples to apples.

Do you travel outside Denver?

Yes, all over Colorado and to destination weddings. I've played in Italy, Mexico, Iceland, Ibiza and New York. Travel details go in your quote up front. See mountain weddings.

Are you insured?

Yes. I carry liability insurance, my gear is insured, and I can send your venue a certificate of insurance (COI).

Planning and music

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book weddings about a year out. Parties and corporate events usually book a few weeks to a few months ahead. If your date is soon, reach out anyway. If I'm open, I'm happy to make it work.

Will you be our DJ, or someone else?

Me. The person you plan with is the person behind the decks. No roster and no surprise replacement.

Do you play a preprogrammed mix, or mix live?

I mix live, every time. We plan the music together ahead of time, but the order happens in the room, based on who's dancing. A preprogrammed mix is locked before anyone walks in, so it can't react when a song isn't landing. If you're deciding between a playlist and a DJ, here's my honest take on playlists vs. a live DJ.

How do we share our music with you?

We set up shared Spotify playlists. Give me must-plays, a do-not-play list and a direction for everything else. Your do-not-play list is a hard rule.

Do you take requests?

That's your call. Anyone can request, just the wedding party, or nobody. I'll keep the vibe right either way.

Clean or explicit music?

Your rule, decided ahead of time. Most couples go clean early and loosen up late. Corporate events and school dances stay clean all night. More in clean vs. explicit.

Can you make a custom edit of a song?

Yes. I can extend a walk-down song, shorten a first dance, blend two songs or drop a lyric you don't want your family to hear. See wedding music, moment by moment.

Do you MC?

Yes, as much or as little as you want. Grand entrance, dinner, toasts, first dance and send-off. Here's what a wedding MC actually does.

Ceremony, sound and lighting

Can you do our ceremony sound?

Yes. Speakers, a wireless mic for your vows and a clip-on mic for your officiant. Ceremony coverage is part of the Full Experience and Extended Experience packages.

What if our ceremony spot has no power?

I bring battery-powered speakers and wireless mics, so the ceremony can happen at an overlook, a farm field or a backyard corner. Read the Boulder Flower Farm story for a real example.

How many speakers will we need?

It depends on the space more than the headcount. I size it for you, and if you're curious, here's how I think about speakers.

Can you work with our band?

Yes. I can cover cocktail hour, dinner, band breaks and the late-night dance floor, and I'll coordinate the setup with the band directly. I often run through the band's system for my sets.

Do you bring lighting?

Standard lighting is included. Weddings often keep it clean and simple. Parties tend to want uplighting, lasers and haze. If you want something I don't own, I can rent it.
