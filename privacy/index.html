Privacy

Privacy policy

Plain English: what happens to the information you send me through this site. Last updated October 30, 2026.

Who I am

This site is run by NOSIRROT LLC, doing business as DenverEventDJ, based in Denver, Colorado. You can reach me at josh@denvereventdj.com or (720) 445-5369.

What I collect

When you fill out the contact form, I receive what you enter: usually your name, email, phone number, event date, venue and details about your event. The form is processed by Formspree, which delivers your message to me by email.

This site also uses Google Analytics to understand how people find and use it, such as which pages are visited and whether the contact form was submitted. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information like your browser, device and general location. It doesn't tell me who you are.

How I use it

I use your information to reply to you, put together a quote and plan your event. That's it. I don't sell your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Who else sees it

Only the services that run this site and its form: Formspree for form delivery, Google Analytics for site statistics and GitHub, which hosts the site. If you book, I may share event details like your timeline with your other vendors so the day runs smoothly, and only with your OK.

Your choices

Email me any time to ask what I have, fix it or delete it. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out tools for Analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
