Good questions. Quick answers.

What's included in a package?

Me as your DJ, music planning, MC support, professional sound and standard lighting appropriate for the event. We'll confirm the setup and schedule together before you book.

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely. Whether it's a reception, a company party or a wedding with multiple spaces, I'll help plan the sound, music and equipment around your event. If we need something I don't already own, we can look at rental options and include the details in your quote.

Do you travel outside Denver?

Yes. I DJ weddings and events across Colorado and beyond. We'll discuss any travel details in your quote before you book.

Can you handle both our ceremony and reception?

Yes. Full Experience is designed for ceremony-to-reception coverage. I'll go over the venue, microphones and sound setup with you so everything is planned ahead of time.
